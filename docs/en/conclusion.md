# 🎉 Conclusion

**Congratulations!** If you have followed all of these steps, you now own a working *maimai DxNALE* cabinet, ready for online play!

---

## Going further

The guide is over, but a few appendices are there if you need them:

* **[Official manuals](official-manuals.md)** - the original maintenance manuals (*maimai DX* and *maimai PiNK*), to look up a precise part number or a manufacturer procedure.
* **[Wiring diagrams](wiring-diagrams.md)** - the wiring diagrams extracted from those manuals in high resolution, to find a wire without having to zoom around in a PDF that is several dozen MB.
* **[Error codes](error-codes.md)** - the error codes documented in the official manuals, with their cause and the manufacturer's recommended remedy.
* **[Understanding serial protocols](understanding-serial-protocols.md)** - a short course for those discovering UART, RS-232, RS-485, JVS or USB-CDC, who want to understand how these devices "talk to each other".
* **[Glossary](glossary.md)** - a reminder of the technical terms used throughout the guide, in case a term still trips you up.
* **[Useful links](useful-links.md)** - external resources that do not directly concern the conversion, but whose content may prove useful.
