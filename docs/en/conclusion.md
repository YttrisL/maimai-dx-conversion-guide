## Going further

The guide is over, but a few appendices remain within reach if needed:

* **[Official manuals](official-manuals.md)** - the original maintenance manuals (*maimai DX* and *maimai PiNK*), to look up a precise part number or a manufacturer procedure.
* **[Wiring diagrams](wiring-diagrams.md)** - the wiring plates extracted from those manuals in high resolution, to find a wire without having to zoom into a PDF of several dozen MB.
* **[Understanding serial protocols](understanding-serial-protocols.md)** - a short course for those discovering UART, RS-232, RS-485, JVS or USB-CDC, who want to understand how these devices "talk to each other".
* **[Glossary](glossary.md)** - a reminder of the technical terms used throughout the guide, in case a word still escapes you.
* **[Useful links](useful-links.md)** - external resources that do not directly concern the conversion, but whose content may prove useful.
