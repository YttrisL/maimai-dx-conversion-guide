# 📚 Official manuals

This page lists the official manuals referenced throughout this guide.

* [*maimai DX* instruction manual](../resources/pdfs/maimai-dx-instruction-manual-full.pdf): the official maintenance manual for the cabinet in its ***maimai DX*** version.
    * [English translation](../resources/pdfs/maimai-dx-instruction-manual-full-en.pdf): an **unofficial** English version of the manual above, **translated by an LLM**. It keeps the original layout and page numbering, so the page references in this guide still apply, but it may contain errors or approximations: when in doubt, the Japanese manual is authoritative.
* [*maimai DX* service manual](../resources/pdfs/maimai-dx-service-manual-full.pdf): the official *service manual* for ***maimai DX***, focused on the cabinet's software rather than its hardware.
* [*ALLS HX2* service manual](../resources/pdfs/alls-hx2-service-manual-full.pdf): the official *service manual* for the ***ALLS HX2*** PC, the central PC used by *maimai DX* (see [step 1](step-1-alls-and-psu.md)).
* [maimai PiNK manual](../resources/pdfs/maimai-pink-manual-full.pdf): the official maintenance manual for the cabinet in its **maimai PiNK** version, a revision earlier than *FiNALE*. However, there is no notable hardware difference between PiNK and *FiNALE*.

The *maimai DX* and *maimai PiNK* manuals each contain, in their last pages, a complete wiring diagram of the cabinet. A high-resolution extraction of those diagrams, quicker to consult than the full PDFs, is available on the [Wiring diagrams](wiring-diagrams.md) page.
