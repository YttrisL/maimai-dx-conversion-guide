# Official manuals

This page lists the official manuals referenced throughout this guide.

* [maimai DX manual](../resources/pdfs/maimai-dx-manual-full.pdf): the official maintenance manual for the cabinet in its **maimai DX** version, the one this guide proposes converting to.
* [maimai PiNK manual](../resources/pdfs/maimai-pink-manual-full.pdf): the official maintenance manual for the cabinet in its **maimai PiNK** version, a revision earlier than *FiNALE*. However, there is no notable hardware difference between PiNK and FiNALE.

Each of these manuals contains, in its last pages, a complete wiring diagram of the cabinet. A high-resolution extraction of those plates, quicker to consult than the full PDFs, is available on the [Wiring diagrams](wiring-diagrams.md) page.
