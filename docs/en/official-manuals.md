# Official manuals

This page lists the official manuals referenced throughout this guide.

* [maimai DX Instruction manual](../resources/pdfs/maimai-dx-instruction-manual-full.pdf): the official maintenance manual for the cabinet in its **maimai DX** version, the one this guide proposes converting to.
* [maimai DX service manual](../resources/pdfs/maimai-dx-service-manual-full.pdf): the official *service manual* for **maimai DX**, focused on the cabinet's software rather than its hardware.
* [ALLS HX2 service manual](../resources/pdfs/alls-hx2-service-manual-full.pdf): the official *service manual* for the **ALLS HX2** PC, the central PC used by *maimai DX* (see the [ALLS and PSU](step-1-alls-and-psu.md) step).
* [maimai PiNK manual](../resources/pdfs/maimai-pink-manual-full.pdf): the official maintenance manual for the cabinet in its **maimai PiNK** version, a revision earlier than *FiNALE*. However, there is no notable hardware difference between PiNK and FiNALE.

The *maimai DX* and *maimai PiNK* manuals each contain, in their last pages, a complete wiring diagram of the cabinet. A high-resolution extraction of those plates, quicker to consult than the full PDFs, is available on the [Wiring diagrams](wiring-diagrams.md) page.
