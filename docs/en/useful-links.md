# Useful links

This page lists external resources that do not directly concern the *FiNALE -> DX* conversion, but whose content may nonetheless prove useful.

* **[ADX Controller (Yuancon) purchasing guide](https://github.com/maimai-dx/ADX-Controller-Guide/blob/master/document/Purchasing.md)**: Yuancon's purchasing guide for its ADX Controller. Although it does not concern us directly, the last chapters of its content remain useful for the various button models available as well as their maintenance.
* **[Guide to imaging an ALLS SSD (redive.world)](https://redive.world/guide/alls-image.html)**: although this guide is aimed at *Wacca* and not *maimai DX*, it remains useful, notably for its method of imaging an ALLS SSD without triggering its protections. Its other pages also contain interesting information and photos about how certain standard Sega PCBs work, such as the IO4.
* **[ALLS references and specifications (arcade-docs)](https://gitea.farewell.dev/Cool_Tools/arcade-docs/src/branch/main/sega/boards.md)**: lists the part numbers of several ALLS as well as their technical specifications.
* **[Official HanDevice documentation](https://handevice.notion.site/)**: the official documentation of HanDevice, the maker of the HDX touchscreen. It is in Chinese, but remains readable via the browser's built-in translator, and offers, among other things, a page dedicated to installing its touchscreen in a *maimai FiNALE* cabinet.
